Jeongyeon modifications: selected exact casefolded Oxford headword spellings from the
English large table, converted proportions to the source's rounded Zipf scale,
and associated them with stable application IDs. Missing values and phrases
remain unscored. No source corpus passages or complete frequency list copied.
Case, meanings and grammatical parts of speech are not separated by this source.
